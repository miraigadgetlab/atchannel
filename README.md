I wanted to make a forum-type site, taking the name from @channel in Steins;Gate (Steins;Gate is peak). I actually started out trying to recreate the exact version from the anime/visual novel, but then I realized it was super impractical and just felt plain old—not even retro, just straight-up ancient and barely had any features from what I saw. So I just took the name, messed around doing my own thing, and came up with this site. It's open source because why not?
